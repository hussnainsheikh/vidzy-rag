# Vidzy Product FAQ

## Which video sources can I use?

You can configure YouTube, Vimeo, or a direct media URL. Vidzy stores and plays the referenced source; the video-creation form does not upload the source video file itself.

## Can I put a CTA inside a video?

Yes. Create a button, link, or image CTA, choose its default time, and assign it to videos. You can override its time for each assigned video.

## Can Vidzy collect leads during playback?

Yes. A timed lead gate pauses playback and can collect email, name, phone, and one custom field. Email, phone, and the custom field can be configured as required where applicable.

## Can I customize the player?

Yes. Branding presets control colors, play buttons, controls, and CTA treatment. Videos can also have a custom thumbnail, logo watermark, and positioned image overlays.

## Can videos be password protected?

Yes. A protected player requires the viewer to enter the correct password before the media is shown.

## How do I add a video to my website?

Open the video's Embed tab and copy the iframe markup. A direct share link is available in the same place.

## Does Vidzy provide video analytics?

Yes. Vidzy records player events and reports counts, completion rate, timestamp activity, and calculated replay hotspots. Its heatmap is event activity rather than distinct-viewer or continuous-watch measurement.

## Can I export leads?

Yes. Lead records can be filtered and exported as CSV, spreadsheet-compatible HTML, or PDF.

## Does Vidzy send leads directly to a CRM?

No automatic CRM delivery is implemented in this repository. Export leads for external processing.

## Can I use a custom domain?

Custom-domain setup is implemented for Premium and Enterprise plan assignments. It requires CNAME and TXT verification and is used for links only after its status becomes active.

## Can I use a video preview in email?

Yes. Vidzy generates a linked animated preview and provides copyable email HTML, with a static-image fallback for Outlook when a thumbnail is available.
