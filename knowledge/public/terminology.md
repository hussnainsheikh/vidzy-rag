# Vidzy Terminology

## Video

A Vidzy video is a stored configuration that points to a YouTube, Vimeo, or direct media URL. It includes playback settings and can reference engagement and branding resources.

## Player

The Vidzy player is the public playback surface. It selects a provider-specific player, renders Vidzy controls and overlays, and sends interaction events to the backend.

## Branding preset

A reusable theme containing player colors, template choice, CTA style, play-button style, and control-bar options. A video may select one preset and may also carry video-specific branding overrides.

## CTA

A reusable call-to-action assigned to one or more videos. It appears at a configured playback time and links to an HTTP or HTTPS destination.

## Popup

A timed message displayed over the player. Configured popup types are CTA, announcement, and offer; CTA popups can include a linked button.

## Lead-capture gate

A timed form that pauses playback and can collect email, name, phone, and one custom field. Required fields determine whether a viewer may skip the gate.

## Image overlay

An image layered over a video. It can use a corner or custom position, size and stacking order, and optional playback start/end times.

## Impression, view, and completion

An impression is recorded when an unlocked player configuration loads. A view is recorded once when playback begins in a player session. A completion is recorded when the provider reports that playback ended.
