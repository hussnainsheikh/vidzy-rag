# Player Customization

Vidzy provides reusable branding presets plus per-video visual assets.

## Branding presets

A preset can define a name, template, primary and secondary colors, filled or outline CTA treatment, one of eleven play-button designs, and control-bar skin, icon, color, and fill choices. The player applies the selected preset to its controls, play button, and branded actions.

## Video-specific assets

A video may upload its own thumbnail and logo watermark. These uploads accept JPEG, PNG, or WebP files up to 5 MB. A video-specific logo overrides the preset logo.

## Image library and overlays

Users can add images by URL or upload JPEG, PNG, WebP, or GIF files. Personal images are visible to their owner; administrators can add global images visible to all users.

Selected images can be overlaid in a corner or at custom percentage coordinates. Overlay size is clamped by the player to 5–60 percent, and stacking order is clamped to 0–1000. An overlay can appear before playback and/or during playback, with optional start and end times.
