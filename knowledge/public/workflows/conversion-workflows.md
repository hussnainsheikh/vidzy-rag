# Conversion Workflows

## Reusable engagement assets

Create a CTA, popup, or lead-capture gate once, assign it to selected videos, and specify a default timestamp. Each video assignment may override the default without changing the reusable asset.

## CTA flow

At the scheduled playback time, the CTA appears. The viewer can dismiss it or click it. Clicking records a CTA event, opens the destination, and removes that CTA from the current display.

## Popup flow

At the scheduled time, a popup appears. The viewer may close it; CTA-style popups can open a configured destination.

## Lead flow

At the scheduled time, playback pauses and the gate appears. Required fields are validated in the player. A successful submission records the lead and resumes playback if it was previously playing. When no field is required, skipping records a skipped lead-capture event but does not create a lead record.
