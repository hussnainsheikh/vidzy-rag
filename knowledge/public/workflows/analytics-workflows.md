# Analytics Workflow

## Review account performance

Use the main dashboard or analytics list to compare videos by impressions, views, completions, completion rate, average event-timestamp percentage, and engagement-event counts. The dashboard also compares the latest seven-day period with the previous seven days for impressions, views, and completions.

## Inspect a video

Open a video's analytics view to review its event breakdown, timestamp heatmap, replay-hotspot calculation, CTA-click count, and lead-capture event count.

Treat retention and watch-time distribution visualizations cautiously: those two dashboard charts are locally estimated from aggregate values rather than returned as measured session data by the analytics API.

## Manage captured leads

Open Leads to search across contact, video, gate, phone, custom-field, and date information. Apply filters before exporting the matching records, or export all records. CSV and spreadsheet-compatible exports are generated in the browser; PDF is generated by the backend.
